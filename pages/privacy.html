Privacy Policy

Read how this site handles your information

Café Core is a web experience: most tools and pages run in your browser. Optional sign-in with Discord lets you access certain signed-in features. We do not sell personal data. This policy describes what may be stored on your device, what our servers handle when you sign in, and how third-party services fit in.

Preferences such as sidebar layout and reduced motion may be saved in your browser's local storage so they persist between visits. Tool inputs (sensitivity, DPI, hardware picks, and similar) generally stay in the page or in local storage unless you clear them; they are not uploaded to Café Core servers for basic calculator and utility use.

During Discord sign-in, your browser may briefly use session storage to remember that a login is in progress. That flag is removed when sign-in completes or is cancelled.

Sign-in is optional. When you choose "Sign in with Discord," you are redirected to Discord's authorization page. We request the identify scope only (enough to recognize you and show your profile in the site UI). We do not request your email, messages, servers, or friends list through this integration.

After Discord approves access, our auth server exchanges the authorization code for a short-lived access token, reads your Discord user profile once, and then discards that token. We do not store Discord access or refresh tokens on our servers.

We keep a signed session cookie (cafe_core_session) on our auth origin (for example, when the static site uses a hosted API). The cookie is HttpOnly and typically lasts up to 30 days or until you sign out. It contains a tamper-resistant payload with your Discord user id, username, display name, and avatar URL so the site can show who is signed in and authorize limited actions. Signing out clears this cookie.

Discord's own privacy policy and terms apply to your Discord account and to data Discord processes during login. See discord.com/privacy.

When auth and site APIs are enabled, our server may process your session to answer requests such as "who is signed in?"

Shared site data (such as Black-Listed scrim win/loss counts) is stored on the server, not in your browser's local storage. Visitors receive current values via HTTP and, when connected, a WebSocket feed for live updates.

Standard connection data (IP address, user agent, timestamps) may appear in hosting or platform logs for security and operations, consistent with our provider's policies.

Tool data such as hardware lists and benchmark charts are loaded from this site's static files in your browser. Fonts, icons, and Discord avatar images may be loaded from content delivery networks; those providers may log standard connection data according to their own policies.

You can use most of Café Core without signing in. You can sign out at any time to remove the session cookie from your browser. You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings to remove on-device preferences and session data.
