Privacy Policy

Read how this site handles your information

Overview

Café Core is a client-side web experience: tools and pages run in your browser. We do not operate user accounts or sell personal data. This policy describes what may be stored locally, what leaves your device, and how third-party services fit in.

Information stored on your device

Preferences such as sidebar layout and reduced motion may be saved in your browser's local storage so they persist between visits. Tool inputs (sensitivity, DPI, hardware picks, and similar) stay in the page unless you clear them; they are not uploaded to a Café Core server because none is required for the site to work.

Third-party services

Some features request public data from other providers (for example, pro player settings used in benchmark charts). Those requests go from your browser directly to the third party under their terms and privacy policies. The site also loads fonts and icons from content delivery networks; those providers may log standard connection data such as IP address according to their own policies.
