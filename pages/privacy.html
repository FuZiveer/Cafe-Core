Privacy Policy

How Café Core handles information

Café Core is a personal gaming site: sensitivity and hardware tools, team pages (including Black-Listed), lineups, and related content. Most of the site runs in your browser. Optional Discord sign-in unlocks a few signed-in features. We do not sell personal information.

Settings such as sidebar layout and motion preferences may be saved in your browser so they persist between visits. Calculator and tool inputs usually stay on your device unless you clear site data. Routine tool use does not upload those inputs to our servers.

Sign-in is optional. If you connect Discord, you are sent to Discord's login screen. We receive only basic profile information needed to show that you are signed in and to allow limited site actions. Sign out removes your session on our side. Discord's policies apply to your Discord account: discord.com/privacy.

Some pages load shared team data from our online service—for example scrim win/loss counts and roster details. When you open those pages, your browser requests the current values. Authorized users may update certain shared data while signed in.

The Black-Listed roster may show Rainbow Six Siege ranks and operator usage from Stats.cc. We request that information on the server using player profiles linked on the team page, then display it on the site. We do not need your Ubisoft password, and visitors do not send their own game credentials to Café Core for that feature.

Hosting providers may keep standard technical logs (such as connection time and browser type) for security and reliability.

The site may load fonts, icons, operator images, and similar assets from other hosts. Links to Discord, Stats.cc, or community pages take you to those services under their own terms.

You can browse most of the site without signing in. You can sign out anytime, or clear cookies and site data in your browser to remove local preferences and sessions.
