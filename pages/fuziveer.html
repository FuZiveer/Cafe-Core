Who I am

I'm FuZiveer — a competitive gamer and developer who builds tools that actually help in-game. I started in the fast, scrappy world of .io games online, and I'm now bringing that same mindset to bigger titles and a sharper home base: Café Core.

How long I've been coding

I've been coding since 2021. Most of that time went into useful projects for .io games — small utilities and tweaks that made playing online smoother, smarter, and more fun. That's where I learned to ship fast and focus on what players actually need.

Why I'm building Café Core

Competitive gaming pushed me to care about precision — sens, strats, gear, and clarity under pressure. Café Core is where I combine that experience with the coding skills I picked up in .io games and aim them at bigger games: one place for tools, strats, and community-built stuff that keeps up with how I play now.

How I build

Ideas usually start as something that annoyed me mid-match — a setting buried three menus deep, a workaround I kept repeating. I prototype quickly, run it in ranked, and only keep what still feels obvious after a week. Small surface, no fluff: if it doesn't save time when you're focused on the round, it doesn't belong in the build.
